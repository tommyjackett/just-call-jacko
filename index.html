the why —

So why is the whole neighbourhood telling you to call Jacko?

Jacko's real name is Michael Jackett. In February 2025, his four-year-old daughter Polly was diagnosed with brain cancer. The next morning, she was in surgery for 14 hours.

What followed was the hardest year of their lives — chemo at the Royal Children's, then 12 weeks of proton radiation therapy in the US, then home for a long road of recovery.

In Feb 2026, Polly's first post-treatment scan came back all clear. She's five now. Still flashing peace signs. Still fierce.

But "all clear" doesn't mean "all done." Polly lives with Posterior Fossa Syndrome — ongoing mobility, balance and regulation challenges. Both Michael and his wife Claire are full-time carers. Neither can go back to a regular job. The family has MRI scans every three months for the next two years.

Just Call Jacko is how he keeps doing what he's always done — fixing things for people — while being there for his kid. Book him, and the money goes straight to keeping his family standing.
