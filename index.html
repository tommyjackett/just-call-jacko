the why —

Why we're doing this.

Mikey — Jacko to everyone who knows him — is a dad of three kids. His youngest, Polly, has spent the last twelve months fighting a brain tumour. Surgery, chemo, three months in the US for proton radiation, and a bell-ringing flight home in January with Polly in remission.

Both Mikey and his wife Claire have stepped out of full-time work to carry Polly through her recovery, keep the household running, and be there for all three kids — with five years of monthly monitoring still ahead. That's the reality, and it's not going away quickly.

Rather than hand out a GoFundMe link, Mikey's doing what he's always done — picking up his tools. Book him for a day. $500, flat. The money goes straight to his family. That's it.

No handout. Just a mate, a ute, and a list — sorted.
